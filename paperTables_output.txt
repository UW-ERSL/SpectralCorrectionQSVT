NON-MONOTONIC FIDELITY IN K -- mode-resolved explanation, 2D Poisson
REBUTTAL ONLY -- the paper states the mechanism qualitatively in Sec. 5.3.8;
the Var_w(r) vs 1-F match below is the evidence, carried in the rebuttal.
==============================================================================================================
  Load energy concentration (uniform load): mode 1 carries 99.05% of ||A^-1 b||^2;
  the 10 lowest modes carry 99.93%; the 32 lowest carry 100.00%.

  Writing r(lam) = lam p(lam) - 1, the QSVT state is the exact solution
  modulated mode-by-mode by (1 + r).  A CONSTANT r is a pure rescaling and
  costs no fidelity at all; only the SPREAD of r across energy-carrying modes
  does.  To second order  1 - F  ~=  energy-weighted variance of r.

  READING.  The base polynomial p0 has a LARGE but nearly UNIFORM residual
  across the energy-carrying modes (mean r = -0.197, std r 2.0e-02), which is almost a pure
  rescaling -- hence its deceptively high fidelity 0.99937 despite a
  20% error.  Correcting a single eigenvalue sets r = 0 on the mode
  carrying 99% of the energy while leaving r ~ -0.20 on the rest: the MEAN
  improves by orders of magnitude but the SPREAD grows, so fidelity DROPS.
  Fidelity only recovers once K covers essentially all energy-carrying modes.

  CONSEQUENCE FOR THE PAPER: fidelity is a poor headline metric here because
  it is blind to the uniform part of the error.  The relative compliance error
  (Table 5) is sensitive to it; it is NOT monotone in K, and the useful
  statement is the improvement at the K the adaptive algorithm selects.
